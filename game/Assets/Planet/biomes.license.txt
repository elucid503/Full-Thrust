Derived from HYBMAP_IGBP_2020_LC.tif, HYBMAP version 1.0 (2022).
Zaichun Zhu, Yuhang Luo, Weiqing Zhao, Muyi Li, Jun Chen, Pengjun Zhao.
HYBMAP: Global hybrid land-cover maps from 2000 to 2020 with a resolution
of approximately 1 km. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.6717123

Changes: land-cover classes mapped to authored vegetation, tree-cover,
aridity and permanent-ice fractions; resampled to 4096 x 2048. Southern
polar coverage, absent below 60S in this source, is modeled as ice.
Generated by tools/landscapes.py. These controls are game scenery, not a
survey of individual trees, local soil composition, or contemporary land use.

Elevation remains NOAA ETOPO 2022. Settlement light distribution remains
NASA Earth Observatory's 2012 Suomi NPP VIIRS composite, courtesy of
Chris Elvidge (NOAA National Geophysical Data Center).
